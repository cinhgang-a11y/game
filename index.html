MASCOTKART

Every track is grown from a seed. Pick a mascot, pick a kart, race.

How to play

W/↑ gas · S/↓ brake/reverse · AD/←→ steer
Space/Shift hold while turning to drift. Sparks turn blue, then orange. Let go for a mini-turbo.
E/X use item · R back on track · C camera · Esc/P pause · M mute
Hit orange arrow pads for a boost and ? boxes for items: 🔥 Turbo, 🍌 Banana, 🫧 Shield.
Rocket start: press gas just after 2 shows. Too early and you'll stall.
Finish in the top 3 to unlock the next track.
